Function 01 · Conceptual Engine

The Problem & The Campus Loop

Replacing chaotic WhatsApp groups and generic social networks with a verified workflow.
✕

The Broken Status Quo

  • Students have ideas but lack critical skills (e.g. Python coder needing UI/UX).
  • Searching through fragmented WhatsApp chats yields no verifiable proof.
  • LinkedIn is too broad, impersonal, and unsuited for campus hackathon team assembly.
  • Nobody knows who on campus has specific skills and is actually available.
✓

The 5-Step Campus Loop

01. Project Idea Posted with Goal
02. Target Roles & Skills Specified
03. Smart AI Candidate Match & Score
04. Portfolio Proof Inspected (GitHub/Figma)
05. Collaboration Request & Team Formed 🚀

Function 08 · Security & Workflow

Privacy-First Collaboration Requests

Protecting personal contact information until mutual acceptance.
1

Private Invitation

Sender selects specific project post and writes personalized note explaining role. Phone numbers are hidden.

2

Accept / Decline

Receiver reviews project scope and portfolio of sender. Can either Accept or Decline with zero spam risk.

3

Contact Unlock

Upon mutual acceptance, verified campus email and communication links unlock automatically.

✓ Result: Clean spam-free ecosystem compliant with campus student data privacy guidelines.
